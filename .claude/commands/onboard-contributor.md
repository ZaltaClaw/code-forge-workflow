---
description: Walk a new contributor through their first PR
---

Welcome a new contributor. Ask:

1. What's their GitHub handle?
2. Do they have repo access yet? (Send them to the repo invite if not.)
3. Walk them through `docs/ONBOARDING.md` step by step — but pause after each section to confirm they're unblocked.
4. After they're set up, suggest a small first-PR target from the "one-line PR" section.

Be warm. They're new.
